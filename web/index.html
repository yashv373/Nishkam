How it works

Three steps.
The same ones you'd do at work.

01 · Write

Real problems

Verilog in a proper editor. Problems written by chip designers, on the things interviews and tape-outs actually test.

02 · Prove

Checked, not sampled

No hand-picked test vectors. A solver tries every input sequence for 20 cycles. If you're wrong, you see the exact cycle.

03 · Harden

Take it to silicon

Send a solved design through synthesis, placement, routing and signoff with LibreLane on gf180mcuD, on your own machine.

Grading

Every input.
Every cycle.

Your design and a reference are joined and handed to Yosys' SAT solver, running in your browser. It looks for any sequence of inputs that makes them disagree. When it finds one, you get that exact trace, not "test 7 failed".
