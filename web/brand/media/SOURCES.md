# Hero video source

hero.mp4 / hero.webm / hero-poster.jpg: a 16 s pencil-sketch (10 s source slowed 1.6x) animation of the chip flow
(spec, architecture, RTL, synthesis, PD, GDS, foundry, wafer, phone), generated with Google Gemini
at the owner's request (2026-10-14). We upscaled it to 1920x1080 with a light sharpen, inverted it to black, tinted the lines brand blue,
dropped the audio and added a fade at the loop point with ffmpeg. The watermark disappears in the contrast curve.
