5. Motion

✗ Don't: 600 ms, bouncy
My profile
✓ Do: 200 ms, ease-out, opacity + transform
My profile

#19[std: Material motion] Hover 100-150 ms, menus 200-250 ms, panels 300 ms at most. Animate only transform and opacity. Toggle "Reduce motion" at the top: both menus snap open and the skeletons stop pulsing.

#20[std: WCAG][ours] All motion off under prefers-reduced-motion. On working screens motion only explains a change; showpiece effects belong on the landing page. This page obeys it (try the toggle).
